The story behind
every shipment

Imex Clearing & Logistics Private Limited is one of Kanpur’s leading International freight forwarder and consolidator, specializing in multi-modal shipments from/to India. We provide the complete ambit of logistic services. The CONTAINER CORPORATION OF INDIA LIMITED for our outstanding performance in this field has awarded us for third time in a row.

Being established in 1994, Foundation of “Imex Clearing” was the ultimate realization of the dream of Mr. Suresh Kumar Mishra. Who initially started air freights and custom clearance business to many challenging destinations all over the world. He made this challenge his specialty and later diversified and created a distinct brand image.
